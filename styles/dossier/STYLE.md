# Investigation File - style guide

> A dark case file: one piece of evidence at a time, a stamp and a headline on a bar, and a red thread tying places together. For the puzzle and the questions "why did it fail?" and "who decided, and why?".
> (The video itself is Arabic: every word shown on screen is Arabic, in a heavy Kufi face.)

## 1. The essence, and what it is not
- **A dark ground with a very faint grid**, like a detective's table: no glow, no coloured light.
- **Evidence yellow and danger red.** Yellow is for what we know (evidence, number, name) and red for what looks wrong or contradictory. Only two colours plus white.
- **One piece of evidence per screen.** A single shot (a map, a number or a photo) with a headline on a bar over it.
- **Everything has a label.** No photo and no number without a short stamp saying what it is.

Not: a horror film (no shadows, no effects), not a coloured data dashboard, not a crowded news screen.

## 2. Materials and drawing
- The `grid` background at low intensity with no motion. Screen corners carry crop marks (deco corners), plus a thin progress bar at the bottom.
- The headline sits on a `bar` that slides in from the right, in a heavy Kufi face.
- Maps are dark with a gradient of yellow on the place concerned; the marker is a diamond (`diamond`), and between two places a red thread is drawn progressively.
- Photos are borderless and extend to the edge (`bleed`), fading into the ground, with a small yellow stamp on them naming the place or the object.
- Numbers are stacked (`stack`): the large number in yellow with a small white label under it. The number appears once.

## 3. Writing on screen
- A headline of three to five words. Labels of two to four words.
- Captions on a bar (`bar`) with the spoken word inside a yellow box.
- Years with no thousands separator.

## 4. Rhythm and direction
- A slightly faster rhythm (pace 1.1), with hard cuts when moving between pieces of evidence.
- At the climax: the red thread arrives and the last piece of evidence appears, with a short silence before it.
- The closing: a question left hanging on the screen, and the video loops.

## 5. Instructions for the visual director
- A screen = one piece of evidence + a headline on a bar + a stamp. No lists of cards.
- Tie two places together with a red thread when comparing or moving.
- Never repeat the number in the headline and the card together.
