# Teacher's Chalkboard - style guide

> A clean green chalkboard on which the idea is written in chalk: the common belief is written, wiped with one stroke, and the correct one is written in the same place.
> For one lesson and one idea: correcting a common mistake, the origin of a name or word, a problem solved step by step.
> (The video itself is Arabic: every word shown on screen is Arabic, in the Reem Kufi chalk face.)

## 1. The essence, and what it is not
- **A board that was just wiped clean.** One dark green with a light grain: no traces of old lessons, no floating dust, no glow.
- **At most three chalks.** White for the structure, yellow for the one thing the lesson follows, and a pale blue for the second voice (the viewer's question or the counterpart name).
- **Few words in large letters.** The board carries the keywords; the explanation is spoken.
- **A lightly real room.** A thin border around the screen (deco border) that suggests the board frame, nothing more.

Not: a whiteboard with coloured markers, and not a presentation slide (nothing appears with a soft fade; everything is written or wiped).

## 2. Materials and drawing
- The `solid` dark green background, with no motion.
- Writing grows from right to left progressively as if a hand were writing, in the chalk face (Reem Kufi).
- Common mistakes are written, then struck through with one line or wiped, and the correct form is written in its place in yellow.
- Maps are flat, thin white lines on the green, the country concerned in yellow chalk, with a small white label. No card and no shadow.
- Photos (if needed) are circular with no border, as if stuck onto the board.

## 3. Writing on screen
- One large line, and at most one small line under it.
- A list of steps (if any) appears line by line and stays on the board.
- Numbers as plain digits; the year with no thousands separator.

## 4. Rhythm and direction
- A slow, deliberate rhythm (pace 0.85): the writing follows the narration words (a word appears when it is spoken).
- Each board holds one question: "they say X" is written, then wiped with one stroke, then "and the truth is Y".
- No cinematic transitions: cuts are by wiping only.

## 5. Instructions for the visual director
- Build the scene as words on an empty board: no cards and no white boxes.
- Use yellow for the lesson's key once in the scene.
- Never fill the screen with elements: leave the board some empty space.
